# Print, messages and notifications

What leaves the phone is what students, parents and principals actually hold. Every channel the deliver tray reaches is a designed surface.

## Print and PDF

Use `DocumentSheet` for every printed or downloaded output, whatever the content type: lesson plan, worksheet, quiz, rubric, exam paper or report. Nothing prints as raw data.

- **Page.** A4 portrait with 16 × 18mm margins, always black on white in print: no glass, no shadows, no washes. Text stays vector text, with Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Sans and the Noto Sans family for every script embedded. Never print a screenshot.
- **Header.** The school's logo, name and place, then the title (Bricolage 700, 20px). A meta grid carries Class, Subject, Board, Textbook, Chapter, Duration, Date and Language. The board and textbook always appear, so a CBSE sheet and a KSEEB sheet are never confused.
- **Body.** Section titles in Bricolage 600 13px; text in Instrument Sans 12px/1.6, and 1.7 for Indic scripts.
- **Page breaks.** Answer keys, marking schemes and each question set start on a new page.
- **Footer.** "Prepared with SahayakAI · Page 1 of 2". The school's name is on the page; the product is not the headline.

## Parent messages

- **Plain text.** WhatsApp and SMS messages are plain text in the parent's language and script, written warmly and signed by the teacher.
- **No branding.** No product branding, no emoji, and no links unless the school asked for them.
- **Approval first.** Nothing is sent until the teacher approves it in the deliver tray ("Nothing leaves the phone until you tap send.").
- **Summaries.** Assessment summaries for parents use the parent's language for every label, not English, and say what the child can do next.

## Notifications

- **Language.** Titles and bodies are in the teacher's interface language. No emoji, no exclamation marks.
- **Content.** Say what happened and where to find it: "Your worksheet is ready. Open Library." "4 parents confirmed. Open Calls."
- **One meaning, one colour.** A ready output uses the leaf ready language (the orb's state); nothing in a notification is red unless it is urgent.
- **The badge** is a single-colour silhouette of the mark, not the full-colour icon.

## Calls

- **Voice and identity.** Parent calls carry the school's name and a warm, short, blame-free script in the parent's language ("Ask before you advise.").
- **Hours.** They are made only within the school's calling hours.
- **Honesty.** An automated call says so when asked.
- **Teacher's view.** The teacher sees the script, the branches and the log (see "The product, screen by screen", Class and calls).
