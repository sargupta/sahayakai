# Writing for teachers

SahayakAI writes for professionals with little time. Every word should help a teacher decide or act in the minutes between periods.

## Voice

- **A colleague, not a product.** Write as a calm, capable colleague in the staffroom would speak. VIDYA uses the first person ("I'll stay a tap away"); the product addresses the teacher as "you".
- **Dignity.** Teachers are experts in their classrooms. Never pity ("struggling teachers"), never sell at them, never frame them as users to engage. Never describe the audience as "rural"; SahayakAI serves teachers from metros to villages.
- **The teacher keeps the judgement.** VIDYA drafts, suggests and prepares; the teacher decides, edits and sends.

## Patterns

| Situation | Write | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A screen headline | Two periods, one prep gap | Welcome back! Here's your dashboard |
| A task | 4 absentees to call | You have pending tasks |
| Progress | Writing board work in हिन्दी | Processing your request… |
| Done | Sent to the Class 7 parent group | Success! |
| An error | The connection dropped. Your topic is kept; try again. | Error 503 |
| Offline | Voice needs signal. Typing, roll call and saved work keep going. | You are offline |
| An offer | I can build a remedial worksheet for the 4 weak concepts. | Want AI to help? |
| A call script | Warm, short, no blame. Ask before you advise. | Inform the parent of the absence |
| Trust | Nothing leaves the phone until you tap send. | Your data is secure |

## Mechanics

- **Sentence case** for every label, title and button.
- **Short verbs** on actions: Open, Deliver, Edit, Start teaching, Submit attendance, End call.
- **The middle dot** ` · ` joins metadata: "Class 7 · Maths test · 34 sheets".
- **Real specifics.** Counts, classes, times, board, textbook and chapter.
- **Names.** Write SahayakAI as one word with a capital AI. VIDYA always takes capitals. The company appears only as the endorsement "by SARGVISION Intelligence".
- **No emoji, no exclamation marks, no hype** ("magic", "supercharge", "revolutionise", "AI-powered").
- **"Class", not "Grade"**, in Indian contexts.
- **Numbers as digits** in UI ("4 absentees"); write them out in spoken scripts as the language speaks them.

## Truthfulness

- Never invent statistics, testimonials or named people. A sample is labelled as a sample.
- Never claim a capability the product does not ship. Offline queueing, for example, is designed, not live.
- Cite the board, textbook and chapter, and check them against the curriculum data.
