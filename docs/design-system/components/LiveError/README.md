The VIDYA Live failure card: red-edged glass, the alert glyph, what went wrong in VIDYA's words and what she kept.

**Use** at the top of the failed Live screen, above the "What I caught" `LiveTranscript`. The screen has no orb in this state.

**The consumer provides** a first-person `title` ("I couldn’t hear that clearly") and a body that says what she kept and what happens next ("The connection dropped mid-sentence. I’ve kept what I caught.").

**Rules**
- `surface-glass-chip` on a `red-row-border` edge, 22px radius; a 34px `red-500` disc with the white alert glyph; title Bricolage 600 16px and body 13px/1.6, both `red-700`.
- `role="alert"`, so it is announced once when it appears. Use `role="note"` when showing one that is already known.
- Never blame the teacher, never alarm: say what happened, that her words are safe, and the way forward (finish typed, or voice again when the signal returns).
- The failed `LiveStatus` pill says "Couldn’t hear"; the card explains.
