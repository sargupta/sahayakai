Scan answer sheets: the camera on its dark ground, the running tally of sheets, and one action to finish and grade.

Built from the following:
- a `ScreenHeader` with the test and class, an "Experimental" `Pill` and the sheet count;
- the camera frame with its guide and scan line;
- a `CaptureStrip` (latest three sheets and an add tile);
- camera `Chip`s for auto-capture, torch and batch mode;
- the primary `Button` "Finish and grade";
- the camera `Orb` parked above the CTA and the camera `TabBar` on Library.

The orb keeps its colour on the camera ground; only its shadow changes.
