The lesson composer: topic, class, length and board as field cards, what to include, and the curriculum alignment stated before anything is written.

Built from the following:
- `ScreenHeader`;
- `Field` (lg for the topic; md selects for class, length and board, three across);
- small leaf `Chip`s;
- an indigo `NoticeCard` naming board, textbook and chapter;
- a soft `GlassCard` of recent topics;
- the primary `Button`.

The orb parks on the left at the CTA perch and is the way to speak a topic, so the topic card carries no mic. There is no corner glow. The Library tab is active.
