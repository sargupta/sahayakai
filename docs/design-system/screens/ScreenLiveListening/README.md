VIDYA Live, listening: the teacher speaks hands-free; a large saffron orb and a live transcript show VIDYA hearing her.

**The state** VIDYA is listening. The screen takes `wash-live-listening` lit by `glow-live-listening` behind the orb; the pill says "Listening · हिन्दी"; the 164px `LiveOrb` breathes in a halo that swells with her voice; `LiveTranscript` shows her words with a saffron caret.

**How the teacher gets here** Live is the sustained, hands-free conversation. Tapping the parked orb opens the ask-in-place `VoiceSheet`; its "Keep talking" control expands the sheet into this screen. (Holding or dragging the orb only moves it.) On the web, "Talk to VIDYA" starts Live directly.

**While she talks** When she stops speaking, VIDYA starts working (the working screen). Barge-in: whenever she talks over VIDYA, VIDYA stops and returns here. Mute pauses the mic without ending; Type moves to the composer with her words so far; Back collapses Live into the sheet.

**How it ends** End returns her to the page she came from. The conversation, with this transcript, is kept in Library under Conversations; a conversation never creates a Generation. Anything VIDYA makes along the way is saved as its own Generation.
