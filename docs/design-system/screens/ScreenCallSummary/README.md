Call summary: what VIDYA writes back after a call, from what happened to what the teacher does next.

Built from the following:
- a back-only `ScreenHeader` with "Save to record" (`Button` `secondary` `xs`, saffron text) trailing;
- the `SummaryCard` stack, 9px apart: done (the screen's `h1`), Parent response, Concerns raised, Parent commitments, Your action items, Follow-up recommended, and the transcript link;
- `Button`s at `md`: Make the topic sheet (`secondary`) beside Schedule follow-up (`primary`).

The parent's commitments are in their own words; the teacher's action items are verbs. The summary is not saved to the student's record until the teacher taps Save to record.
