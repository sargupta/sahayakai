Exam paper output: a board-blueprint paper with sections and marks, past-paper tags and the marking scheme.

Built from the following:
- a back disc (`ScreenHeader`) and a glass `Pill` with the paper's time and marks ("3 hours · 80 marks");
- the muted `Eyebrow` naming the board's blueprint ("Exam paper · CBSE blueprint"), the title, and the general instructions as its subline;
- an `ActionBar` (Edit · Regenerate · Download · Share, `md`);
- section headings with their marks, each over exam `QuizQuestion`s: a PYQ-tagged multiple choice with the answer marked, and a short answer with an OR alternative and its marking scheme;
- the blueprint's unit weights as `Pill`s, beside the parked orb;
- Print paper as the one primary action with Answer key beside it, and the `TabBar` on Library.

Sections, marks and weights follow the named board's current pattern; a state board paper names its own blueprint.
