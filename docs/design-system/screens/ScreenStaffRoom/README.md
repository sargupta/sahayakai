Staff room: a live room of teachers to ask or share with, the groups you belong to, and what colleagues tried today.

Built from the following:
- `ScreenHeader` at screen level ("Staff room", "42 teachers here now") with a Find teachers button;
- the glass `SearchField` ask bar with the mic disc ("Ask something, or share what worked");
- a sideways `GroupTile` row (`.sk-grouptile-row`) of solid saffron, leaf and indigo tiles;
- `PostCard`s at `size="room"`: a Tried this post with Translate, and a shared worksheet with Save;
- the parked `Orb` and the `TabBar` on Community.

The ask bar's mic dictates into the bar; VIDYA is the orb. Group names mix boards and states.
