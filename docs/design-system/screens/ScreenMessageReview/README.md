Message review: the last step before a parent is contacted, the message in their language with its citation.

Built from the following:
- the page behind (`ScreenHeader` "Parent outreach") dimmed to 34% under a `Sheet`;
- a custom sheet header: "Review before sending" (Bricolage 18/700) over who it goes to, the masked number on record and the language;
- a `MessagePreview` (`tone="inset"`) with its leaf citation;
- `Button`s at `sm`: Regenerate (`quiet`, refresh glyph) full width, then Copy for WhatsApp (`outline`) beside Call parent (`success`, phone glyph).

Call parent is the only filled action and it is leaf, because placing the call is the step that completes the flow. Nothing leaves the phone until it is tapped, and the call goes only to the number on the record, inside the school's calling hours.
