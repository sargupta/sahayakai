Instant Answer, the ask: one question from class, six examples to start from, and the class, subject and language.

Built from the following:
- the `ScreenHeader` page title and subline;
- a large `Field` for the question, multiline, with its mic for dictation into the field;
- a muted `Eyebrow` and a radiogroup of `SuggestionRow`s ("Try one of these"); choosing one puts it in the question;
- three `Field` md selects (Class, Subject, Language) that open `OptionSheet`s;
- a compact `HonestyNote` that says answers come from VIDYA's own knowledge unless a web check is on;
- one primary `Button`, "Get instant answer".

**Where it fits.** Instant Answer is one question and one answer. For back-and-forth, the teacher continues in the conversation (the typed or turn-based thread); VIDYA Live is hands-free voice; the ask-in-place `VoiceSheet` is a quick ask about the current page. Every conversation, typed or Live, is kept in Library → Conversations, never as a Generation.

The examples are everyday and Indian ("Photosynthesis, using how Pongal is made", "Explain gravity with a bullock cart"). The subject follows the question (photosynthesis is Science). The single call to action is the saffron primary; black pills belong to the website only.
