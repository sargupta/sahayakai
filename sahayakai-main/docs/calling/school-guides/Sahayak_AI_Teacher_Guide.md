## 1. Purpose and when to use it

Sahayak AI helps you reach a student's parent quickly, in the parent's own language. You choose a reason, add a short note, and Sahayak AI drafts a message. You then either:

- **Call the parent.** An automated voice assistant phones the parent on your behalf, gives your message and listens to their reply. Afterwards you get a summary and a transcript.
- **Copy for WhatsApp.** You paste the message into WhatsApp and send it yourself.

### Good uses

| Use it for | Example |
|---|---|
| Checking in after absences | A student has missed three days in a row |
| Sharing an academic concern | A low score in a recent unit test |
| A mild behaviour concern | Repeatedly talking during lessons this week |
| Good news | A strong test result, or helping classmates |

### Do NOT use an automated call for

- Safeguarding or child-protection concerns, or anything that suggests a child may be at risk.
- Injuries, illness or emergencies.
- Bereavement or serious family difficulty.
- Serious discipline (possible suspension or exclusion).
- Situations where you are unsure who may be contacted (custody, separated parents).
- A parent who has asked to be contacted personally.

For these, speak to the parent yourself, or follow your school's procedure. Talk to [School support contact] if unsure.

## 2. Step-by-step walkthrough

### Before you start

- Your account must be on a **School Gold** or **School Premium** plan to place calls.
- Each student must have the right **Parent's Phone** and **Parent's Language** saved. Check under **Attendance → your class → Students**.
- Calls can only be made between **09:00 and 21:00 India Standard Time**.

### Step 1: Open the contact window

You can start in either of two places on your class page:

- **Needs outreach today** (at the top of the class page). Students are grouped under **Consecutive absences**, **At-risk academically**, **Behavioral concern** and **Celebrate**. Tap a student's name. The matching reason is selected for you.
- **Students tab → Parent Outreach list.** Tap **Contact** next to the student's name.

The window that opens is titled **Contact [Student]'s Parent**. Under the title you will see the parent's phone number and language. **Check both before going further.**

> If you used this window for the same student in the last 24 hours, it may open straight to the result of that call instead of the first step. This is normal.

### Step 2: Choose a reason

Under **Select reason for outreach:** choose one:

| Reason | Description shown |
|---|---|
| **Consecutive Absences** | Student has been absent for multiple days |
| **Academic Concern** | Grades or performance has declined |
| **Behavioral Concern** | Classroom behavior needs attention |
| **Positive Feedback** | Share an achievement or good news |

Tap **Next: Add Note**.

### Step 3: Check the context and add a note

Depending on the reason, a box shows what the AI will use:

| Reason | What you see |
|---|---|
| Consecutive Absences | **Absent days (AI will reference)**: the recent absent dates. The AI will express concern for the child's wellbeing and will not mention test scores. |
| Academic Concern | **Recent marks (AI will cite lowest)**: up to three recent assessments, lowest first. |
| Behavioral Concern | **Behavioral context (AI will reference your note)**. The AI has no classroom observations, so **your note is essential**. |
| Positive Feedback | **Recent win (AI will celebrate)**: the best recent mark or excellent attendance, if any. |

Type a short, factual note in the box (one or two sentences). For a behaviour concern, describe what happened. For the other reasons the note is optional but makes the message much better.

Tap **Generate Message**. This can take up to about 20 seconds. If you see **Failed to generate**, tap **Generate Message** again.

### Step 4: Review the message

The draft appears in a box.

- **Read all of it.** It is written in the parent's language. **If you cannot read that language, ask a colleague who can before you send or call.**
- The text cannot be edited in this window, and this screen has no **Back** button. If the tone or wording is not right, tap **Regenerate** for a new version. To change the facts, close the window and start again with a better note.
- Check names, dates and marks against your own records.

> **On a call, the assistant speaks a shorter, phone-friendly version** of this message, prepared automatically at the same time. You will not see that version before the call. Check the transcript afterwards.

### Step 5: Send it

You will see two buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy for WhatsApp** | Copies the message. You then open WhatsApp and paste it to the parent yourself. The window shows **Message copied!** No call is made. |
| **Call Parent** (green) | Starts the automated phone call straight away. |

Notes:

- If you do not see **Call Parent**, calling is not switched on for your account. Use **Copy for WhatsApp** and tell [School support contact].
- If you see **Auto-call is not available for [language]. Use WhatsApp copy instead.**, use WhatsApp for this parent.
- If you see a red **Call failed** message, read the reason (see Section 7).

### Step 6: Follow the call

After you tap **Call Parent**, the window shows the call's progress (see Section 6). **You can close the window.** The call continues. To see the result later, open **Contact** for the same student again within 24 hours.

### Step 7: Read the result

When the call ends, read the summary **and** open **View conversation transcript** to check what was actually said. Then act on **Your Action Items** and **Follow-up Recommended** where shown. Tap **Close** when done.

## 3. Writing accurate, respectful, age-appropriate messages

The AI drafts the words, but you are responsible for what is sent.

### Accuracy

- Put only facts you are sure of in your note: dates, scores, what you saw.
- Check that the draft matches the facts. The AI can make mistakes.
- Never guess at reasons ("probably not studying at home"). Describe, don't diagnose.

### Respect

- Assume the parent wants the best for their child.
- Ask, don't accuse: "Is everything all right at home?" rather than "Why is your child not coming?"
- Name something positive about the child, even in a concern message.
- Avoid school jargon and abbreviations.

### Age-appropriateness and dignity

- Describe behaviour in neutral words suitable for the child's age.
- Do not include other students' names, marks or incidents.
- Do not include sensitive information about the child's health, family or finances.
- Keep it short. Over the phone, long messages are hard to follow.

### Good and poor notes

| Poor note | Better note |
|---|---|
| "Lazy, never does homework." | "Missed 3 of the last 5 homework tasks. Does well when she does them." |
| "Very bad behaviour." | "Called out during lessons on Monday and Tuesday. Usually helpful in group work." |
| "Failed maths." | "Scored 6/25 in the unit test on fractions. Happy to share practice sheets." |
| (empty, positive feedback) | "Helped two classmates with fractions during revision." |

## 4. Sample message and conversation

*All names below are fictional.*

**Setting:** Teacher Ms. Meena Rao, Class 6B, Sunrise Public School. Student Aarav Sharma. Reason: Consecutive Absences. Parent's language: English.

**Teacher's note:** "Usually a regular attender; checking if everything is okay at home."

**Drafted message (example):**

> Dear Parent,
>
> I hope you and your family are well. I wanted to reach out because Aarav has been absent from school for the last three days. He is usually very regular, and we have missed him in class. I wanted to check whether everything is all right and whether there is anything we can do to support him.
>
> Please let me know if he needs help catching up on the lessons he has missed.
>
> Warm regards,
> Ms. Meena Rao

**Example call (illustrative only, *awaiting real-phone verification*):**

| Speaker | What is said |
|---|---|
| Recorded greeting | "Namaste. This is an important message from your child's school." |
| Parent | "Hello? Yes?" |
| Assistant | "I'm calling from Sunrise Public School on behalf of Ms. Meena Rao. Am I speaking with Aarav's parent?" |
| Parent | "Yes, I'm his mother. Tell me." |
| Assistant | "Ms. Rao asked me to let you know that Aarav has been away from school for the last three days. He's usually very regular, so she wanted to check that everything is all right, and whether there's any way the school can help." |
| Parent | "He had a fever. He'll come back on Monday." |
| Assistant | "Oh, I'm sorry to hear that. I hope he feels better soon. I'll let Ms. Rao know he'll be back on Monday, and she can help him catch up." |
| Parent | "Okay, thank you." |
| Assistant | "Thank you for your time. We're partners with you in Aarav's success. Goodbye." |
| | *(The call ends.)* |

**What Ms. Rao might see afterwards (example):**

- **Call completed** · 1 min · Parent was cooperative
- **Parent Response:** Mother said Aarav had a fever and will return on Monday.
- **Parent Commitments:** Aarav will return to school on Monday.
- **Your Action Items:** Help Aarav catch up on the lessons he missed.

## 5. What the parent experiences

| Stage | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ringing | The parent's phone rings from the service's calling number (not your personal number). | Awaiting real-phone verification |
| Greeting | A short recording in the parent's language: *"Namaste. This is an important message from your child's school."* (For Odia, this greeting is currently in Hindi.) | Awaiting real-phone verification |
| Introduction | The assistant names the school and you, says it is calling on your behalf, and checks it is speaking with the child's parent. It does not give itself a personal name and does not claim to be you. | Verified in simulation (English) |
| Is it automated? | The assistant says it is an assistant if the parent asks directly. It does not say so otherwise. **[Whether every call should say so up front: TO BE CONFIRMED BY SCHOOL/COMPANY]** | Verified in simulation |
| Your message | The assistant gives your message, essentially as written, in a phone-friendly form. | Verified in simulation (English) |
| Talking back | The parent can ask questions or share concerns, and can interrupt the assistant at any time. The assistant keeps its replies short. | Verified in simulation (English, synthetic speech); real speech not yet verified |
| Questions it cannot answer | Anything beyond this child and this message (fees, admissions, other children): the assistant says it can't help with that and offers a call from you. | AI instruction; not guaranteed |
| Busy parent | If the parent says they are busy or asks to be called later, the assistant offers to have you call at a better time and ends the call. | AI instruction |
| Ending | The assistant says a short, warm goodbye and hangs up. Calls also end automatically after long silence, about six exchanges, or about 10 minutes. | Goodbye-then-hang-up verified in simulation |
| Voicemail | If it reaches an answering machine, it leaves a short message saying the school will try again. | Not yet verified |

**Known issue seen in testing:** after being interrupted, the assistant sometimes introduced itself again. If a parent mentions this, apologise and report it.

## 6. Understanding call status and results

### While the call is in progress

| What you see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Calling...** | The call has been placed. |
| **Ringing...** | The parent's phone is ringing. |
| **Conversation in progress** | The parent answered and is talking with the assistant. |
| **AI agent is talking with [Student]'s parent** | Shown under the status. |
| **[N] exchanges** | How many messages have been exchanged so far. |
| **Summary will appear when the call ends** | Wait, or close the window and come back later. |

### When the call has ended

| What you see | Meaning | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Call completed** | The call connected and ended normally. | Read the summary and transcript. |
| **No answer** | The parent did not pick up. | **Try again** (after 5 minutes), **Send on WhatsApp**, or **Close**. |
| **Line was busy** | The parent's line was busy. | Same as above. |
| **Call could not connect** | The call failed, was declined, or the assistant could not start. | Same as above. Check the phone number. |
| **Message copied!** | You used WhatsApp copy. No call was made. | Paste the message into WhatsApp. |

### Where to find the details

On the **Call completed** screen:

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | The green banner, in whole minutes (rounded up), for example "1 min · 6 exchanges". |
| **Parent's mood** | Same banner: "Parent was cooperative / concerned / grateful / upset / indifferent / confused". This is the AI's judgement. |
| **Parent Response** | A short summary of what the parent said. |
| **Concerns Raised** | Worries the parent mentioned (shown if any). |
| **Parent Commitments** | What the parent said they would do (shown if any). |
| **Your Action Items** | Suggested next steps for you. |
| **Guidance Shared with Parent** | Any suggestions the assistant gave (shown if any). |
| **Follow-up Recommended** | Shown when the AI thinks you should follow up. |
| **Transcript** | Tap **View conversation transcript ([N] messages)** at the bottom. |

Other messages you may see:

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Generating summary...** | The summary is still being prepared. Wait a moment. |
| **Call ended before a conversation could happen** | The parent did not respond long enough for a summary. Try again or use WhatsApp. |
| **Summary isn't available for this call** | The call connected but no summary was made. Read the transcript. |

> **The summary is AI-generated.** Always check it against the transcript before acting on it or recording it.

> **Long calls:** the window stops checking after about 5 minutes, but a call can last up to about 10 minutes. If you do not see a result, close the window and open **Contact** for the same student again.

## 7. When a call does not go to plan

### No answer or busy

1. Don't call again straight away. Parents often miss calls from unknown numbers.
2. You can tap **Try again**, but only after **5 minutes**. Before then you will see **"Recent outreach already exists for this student"**.
3. Or tap **Send on WhatsApp** to copy the same message.
4. Avoid more than [School limit on attempts per day: TO BE CONFIRMED BY SCHOOL] attempts in one day.

### Call could not connect

1. Check the phone number in the student list.
2. Tap **Try again** once.
3. If it fails again, use WhatsApp or another method, and tell [School support contact] if it keeps happening.

### Other messages when you tap Call Parent

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Calls to parents are only placed between 9:00 and 21:00 IST..." | Try again after 09:00 IST. The call is not queued. |
| "This parent asked not to be called again..." | Do not call. See Section 8. |
| "Recent outreach already exists for this student" | Wait 5 minutes. |
| "We could not check this parent's call preferences just now..." | Wait a minute and try again. |
| "PREMIUM_REQUIRED" | Your plan does not include calling. Contact [School support contact]. |
| "Parent phone number is not callable" | Check and correct the number. |

### The assistant misunderstood the parent

- Read the transcript to see what was heard.
- If anything important was misunderstood, **call the parent yourself**.
- Report repeated problems to [School support contact].

### The parent needs a human

Call the parent personally if:

- The summary shows **Follow-up Recommended**, or the parent was **upset** or **confused**.
- The parent asked a question the assistant could not answer.
- The parent asked to speak to you.
- The transcript mentions anything worrying about the child's safety or wellbeing. **Tell your safeguarding lead at once:** [Safeguarding lead contact].

## 8. Do-not-call requests

If a parent says on a call that they don't want to be called again, the assistant:

1. Thanks them and says it has been noted, without arguing or asking why.
2. Ends the call.
3. Records the request against that phone number. *(Verified in simulation. Awaiting real-phone verification.)*

After that:

- Any automated call to that number is **refused**, for you, for other teachers, and for brothers or sisters who share the number. You will see: *"This parent asked not to be called again, so we have not placed the call. Please reach them another way, such as a WhatsApp message or a note home."*
- **Never try to get around this.** Do not change the student's phone number, use another student's record, or ask a colleague to call through the system.
- You may still contact the parent in other ways allowed by your school.
- If a parent tells you in person that they don't want automated calls, **do not use Call Parent for them**, and tell [School support contact]. There is currently no button to record this yourself.
- If a parent later asks to receive calls again, refer it to [School support contact]. **[Process TO BE CONFIRMED BY SCHOOL/COMPANY]**

## 9. Privacy and professional conduct

- Use the feature only for your own students and only for school purposes.
- Keep phone numbers, transcripts and summaries confidential. Don't screenshot or forward them outside approved school channels.
- Don't include other students' information in your note.
- Don't include sensitive health, family or financial details in your note.
- A **written transcript and an AI summary** of each call are saved. Only you can see them in the app. Other access and how long they are kept are **[TO BE CONFIRMED BY SCHOOL/COMPANY]**.
- The call audio is not saved by the calling system.
- When you use WhatsApp copy, you are sending from your own WhatsApp. Follow your school's rules for messaging parents.
- If a parent asks how their information is used, refer them to [School support contact].

## 12. Frequently asked questions

**Does the parent know it's not me on the phone?**
The assistant says it is calling on your behalf from the school. It never claims to be you. It says it is an assistant if asked directly.

**Can I change the words of the message?**
Not directly. You can add or change your note and tap **Generate Message** again, or tap **Regenerate** for a new version.

**Will the parent hear exactly what I see?**
Not word for word. On the call, the assistant speaks a shorter phone version of the same message. Check the transcript afterwards.

**Can I choose the language?**
The language comes from the student's record (**Parent's Language**). To change it, edit the student in the **Students** tab.

**Can I call a different number, for example the father's?**
Only the number saved on the student's record is called. Update the record if the school agrees the number should change.

**Why can't I call again straight away?**
To protect parents from repeated calls, the same student can only be contacted once every 5 minutes. If the previous call failed to connect at all, you can try again sooner.

**Can I call in the evening?**
Yes, until 21:00 IST. After that, calls are refused until 09:00 IST.

**What if the parent asks the assistant something it doesn't know?**
It is told to say it can't help with that and to offer a call from you. Check the summary and transcript and follow up.

**Is the call recorded?**
A written transcript and summary are saved. The calling system does not save the audio.

**What happens if I close the window during the call?**
The call continues. Open **Contact** for the same student again within 24 hours to see the result.

**I can't read the language of the draft. What should I do?**
Ask a colleague who reads that language to check it before you call or send.

**Who do I tell if something goes wrong?**
[School support contact]. For anything about a child's safety: [Safeguarding lead contact].
